Setup

Slash 2 Water and 2 Food in Stores. Then place radiation: roll one die three times, once per band, and fill the dotted circle under that zone.

A1–5 = zone 3,4,5,6,7 · reroll a 6
B1–5 = zone 8,9,10,11,12 · reroll a 6
C1–6 = zone 13…18

Two players wanting a fair race should use the same three ☢ zones and compare final scores.

Your Day, in order

  • Produce from structures built on an earlier day.
  • Build / Barter / Decontaminate — before you roll only.
  • Roll 3 dice.
  • Modify dice as much as you can pay for.
  • Spend all 3 dice — every die goes somewhere.
  • Raiders on days 4, 8 and 12.
  • Upkeep: cross off 1 Food or Water.
  • Cross off the day.

Spending Dice

Each die is spent exactly once, on one section. You may split them however you like — three 1-die plays, or a 2 and a 1, or all three together.

Stuck? Fortify takes a die of any value. Once per day you may also strip 2 unspent dice for 1 Scrap. If you truly cannot place a die, abandon the day: cross off all remaining dice and take 1 Radiation.

Exploring

Zones must be taken in order. Your chosen dice must total the zone number exactly. Mark it and take a Find roll: one die, +1 for each Scout token you spend (max 2).

A ◆ zone (6, 12, 17) also pays Treasure. A ☢ zone costs 1 Radiation but pays Treasure too — that is the whole bargain of going deep.

Changing dice

Repeat as often as you can pay. Tools tick a box instead of spending stores.

Before the roll

Barter once a day: give up any 2 stored resources, take 1 of anything.
Decontaminate: 2 Water erases 1 Radiation.
Build: at most 2 structures ever.

Raiders & Upkeep

On days 4, 8 and 12 they demand 2, then 4, then 6 resources of any kind. Each marked Shelter and each of your first three Fortify boxes cuts the demand by 1 — but never below half, so the least you can pay is 1, then 2, then 3. Cannot pay in full → you are dead.

Upkeep is due every single day: cross off 1 Food or Water. Cannot pay → you are dead.

A 4th Radiation kills you. Each one you carry to the end is −10.

Ending

After day 12, total the score box. Anything still in Stores counts — every resource you burned on upkeep and raiders is score you gave up. That is the game.
